Our Method

The Triple Action Process

Three precision steps. One exceptional result. This is why our name says it all.

1

Pre-Inspection & Pre-Treatment

We assess your carpets, identify stain types, and apply targeted pre-treatment solutions to loosen embedded soils before cleaning begins.

2

Deep Hot Water Extraction

High-powered truck-mount steam cleaning penetrates deep into carpet fibres, flushing out dirt, bacteria, and allergens completely.

3

Finish & Protective Coating

pH-balanced rinse restores fibre integrity. Optional Scotchgard protective layer is applied, leaving carpets fresh, bright, and protected.

✓

Post-Inspection & Your Guarantee

We walk through results with you before we leave. Not satisfied? We come back — at no extra charge. That's our Edmonton promise.
